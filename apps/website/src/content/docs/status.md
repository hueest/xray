title: Status
description: Where xray is in its life — an early proof of concept.
---

xray is an early proof of concept. The capture pipeline, the Vite plugin, the
`<Skeleton>` React adapter, the dev HUD, and the capture-all extension all work and
are dogfooded — including on this site.

**Framework support:** React today. Adapters for other frameworks (and Vite
metaframeworks) are planned; the core is framework-neutral by design.

See the [roadmap](/roadmap/) for what's next and the known limits.
